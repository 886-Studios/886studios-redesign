## Design Context

### Users
Founders evaluating 886 Studios programs, especially founders deciding whether to apply to ikigai Launchpad. They need to quickly understand the offer, credibility, format, and application path without reading a full duplicate of the ikigai website.

### Brand Personality
886 should feel founder-led, direct, sharp, and credible. The tone should be confident without hype, more like a serious founder studio than a startup marketing campaign.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark 886 visual language, restrained purple accents, real founder photos, strong typography, and clear editorial hierarchy. Programs should feel connected to Home and About, not like a separate campaign page. Never use translucent boxes or panels, glassmorphism, or card containers that exist only to group a section. Avoid gold accents, pills, random dividers, excessive panels, cringe taglines, generic accelerator brochure layouts, and obvious AI-looking “dramatic” compositions.

### Design Principles
- Primary conversion is applying to ikigai Launchpad.
- Never use translucent boxes or panels anywhere on the site. Prefer open layouts shaped by spacing, typography, imagery, and simple structural rules.
- Keep only the highest-value details: the standard $100K-for-8% SAFE, 10 weeks, in person in Taipei, mentor office hours, investor intros, and beyond-batch support.
- Make the page more detailed than Home, but not as detailed as the dedicated ikigai website.
- Use real photos intentionally and keep their sizing/alignment controlled.
- Launch Station stays last and visibly secondary.
